# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is JARVIS's owner, operating a persistent autonomous AI organization by exception. The owner needs to understand live operational state, intervene through authenticated controls when necessary, and trace every material decision back to durable evidence.

## Product Purpose

JARVIS Command Center is the local operational interface for observing missions, workers, decisions, evidence, verification, health, recovery, costs, resources, briefings, voice state, and owner directives without becoming a second source of truth.

## Positioning

Unlike a conventional monitoring dashboard, every displayed material result is a projection of JARVIS's append-only event ledger and links back to durable records or evidence. Controls enter the same authenticated directive system used by the Supervisor.

## Operating Context

The owner checks the console during normal autonomous operation and exceptions. Normal operation should report what JARVIS is doing, why it chose the work, what failed, what it learned, and what comes next. STOP and degraded-state visibility must remain understandable even when Hermes, models, connectors, or the network are unavailable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the deterministic Supervisor as the source of operational control and the append-only ledger as the source of truth.
- Show actual measurements where available and explicit `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_AVAILABLE`, `NOT_TESTED`, or `BLOCKED` states otherwise.
- Never fabricate provider results, costs, evidence, acceptance, or live voice capability.
- Owner actions must use authenticated directives and must not bypass policy, capability, audit, or STOP invariants.
- The interface is local-only unless a separately verified deployment is authorized.
- The current V4 completion track is isolated from live JARVIS, Hermes, runtime state, databases, connectors, and external delivery.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: JARVIS Command Center.
- The interface should feel like a futuristic AI operations system with a serious science-fiction character.
- The visual drama must never obscure operational clarity, trustworthy state labels, or evidence provenance.
- Voice and copy should be calm, precise, candid, and operational rather than theatrical or promotional.

## Evidence on Hand

The interface consumes durable missions, agent runs, evidence bundles, verifier verdicts, registered resources, directives, leases, budgets, cost measurements, briefing outbox entries, voice interactions, and the decision timeline from the local Foundry database. Preview content is explicitly synthetic and stored in a disposable database.

## Product Principles

- Durable truth before visual convenience.
- Manage by exception, not approval queue.
- Show why, proof, uncertainty, and next action together.
- Safety controls remain deterministic and independently effective.
- Unknown state is a first-class result, never zero or implied success.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web console must preserve semantic structure, keyboard focus visibility, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive operation on desktop and mobile viewports.
